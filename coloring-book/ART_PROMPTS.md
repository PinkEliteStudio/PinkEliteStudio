# Art prompts for “Made in His Image”

Make each image landscape, ratio about 1.08:1 (about 72:67). The picture area is 7.2 x 6.7 inches, so aim for at least 2175 x 2019 pixels (upscale if your tool makes smaller images).
Save them as `art/01.png` ... `art/30.png`, then rebuild the book.

Check every image: 5 fingers per hand, two feet, eyes and faces look right, no gray shading, no stray text or letters.

**Style (already added to every prompt below):**

> children's coloring book page, black and white line art, thick clean bold outlines, no shading, no grayscale, no color, no text, no words, pure white background, simple shapes, cute cartoon style, joyful, age 5-10, full scene, correct anatomy with five fingers on each hand
